How to Order

Ordering is simple and personal. Here's what to expect.

  1. 1

    Browse

    Look through the shop and find a critter you love — or dream up a custom idea.

  2. 2

    Inquire

    Tap Order / Inquire to send a message, or DM me on Instagram.

  3. 3

    Confirm Details

    I'll reply with pricing, colors, and options, then confirm your order.

  4. 4

    Payment

    Payment is arranged directly with me — easy and secure.

  5. 5

    Made & Delivered

    Your critter is hand-made and shipped or arranged for local pickup.

Custom orders take a little extra time — reach out early for holidays and special occasions so your critter arrives right on schedule.
