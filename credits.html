Design Credits

Credit where credit is due — the patterns behind my critters.

The Cozy Company

Nearly every critter I make starts from a pattern designed by The Cozy Company. The designs are theirs — the elephants, the zebras, the cows, the Itty Bitty Gang, the chunky flowers, and the holiday crews all come from their patterns and video tutorials.

I knit each piece by hand, one stitch at a time, and I choose the yarn and colors — but I didn't dream up these designs, and I don't want that to go unnoticed. Every single listing in my shop names the designer right on the card, and they're linked here too.

Want to make your own?

If you'd rather knit one yourself, go straight to the source. The Cozy Company sells the PDF patterns and video tutorials, plus their own chunky yarn, and they run in-person and private workshops.

Visit The Cozy Company @thecozy_company

What's mine

The handwork, the yarn and color choices, the finishing touches, the photos of my finished critters, and this little website. If you order from me, you're paying for the hours of hand-knitting — not the pattern.

A thank you

Thank you to The Cozy Company for creating patterns that are such a joy to make. I've learned so much from their tutorials, and my shop wouldn't exist without them.
