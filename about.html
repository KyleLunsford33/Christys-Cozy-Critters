Hi, I'm Christy

The maker behind every cozy critter.

Christy's Cozy Critters started with my love of yarn and a heart for making people smile. Every animal, stocking, and flower is hand-knit one stitch at a time right here in North Carolina.

No two critters are exactly alike — and that's the magic. I love adding personal touches and taking on custom orders, so your cozy critter can be as unique as the person it's made for.
